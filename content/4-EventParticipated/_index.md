Throughout the internship program, I had the opportunity to participate in three valuable technology events and technical workshops. Each event represented a unique learning milestone, covering topics ranging from cloud security, containerization, and career development in multinational companies to hands-on experience in a 24-hour AI hackathon.

The following are the events that I attended:

### [Event 1: AWS First Cloud Journey - Workshop (06/06)](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS First Cloud Journey (Cloud Infrastructure, Security & AI on AWS)

&emsp;**Date:** June 6, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Participant

&emsp;**Brief Description:** A series of technical presentations covering modern cloud technologies, including AWS WAF with Machine Learning for Network Intrusion Detection Systems (NIDS), Docker containerization, career development from IT Helpdesk to Senior System Administrator/DevOps, AWS WebSocket architecture for real-time multiplayer games, and GraphRAG using Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Neptune.

&emsp;**Outcomes & Learning:** Gained a solid understanding of Docker image layers for optimizing ETL and Data Warehouse deployment, learned best practices for cloud security and system operations, and explored the application of GraphRAG for advanced knowledge retrieval.

---

### [Event 2: AWS First Cloud Journey - Meetup (13/06)](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS First Cloud Journey Meetup (MNC Culture & DevOps Career)

&emsp;**Date:** June 13, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Participant

&emsp;**Brief Description:** A technical meetup discussing working culture, recruitment processes, and career development in multinational corporations (MNCs), the journey from university student to AWS Partner, and the real responsibilities of DevOps engineers beyond tools and automation.

&emsp;**Outcomes & Learning:** Learned the importance of the **No-Blame Post-Mortem** culture, improved my understanding of **data storytelling**, and developed the mindset of becoming a **System Thinker** capable of viewing engineering challenges from a broader architectural perspective.

---

### [Event 3: Agentic AI Build Week - Hackathon (25/07)](4.3-Event3/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon 2026

&emsp;**Date:** July 25, 2026 

&emsp;**Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Participant

&emsp;**Brief Description:** A 24-hour hackathon focused on building AI-powered MVPs on AWS. Featured projects included **S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D**, an AI-powered crowd monitoring system using Computer Vision; **SA Professional AI Native App**, an AI assistant for cloud architecture design and Infrastructure as Code generation; and **Signal Scout**, a strategic business intelligence platform powered by Agentic AI.

&emsp;**Outcomes & Learning:** Learned the project management principle of **"Scope it Tiny"**, gained practical insights into integrating AI models with AWS services such as Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock Agents, Amazon ECS Fargate, and Amazon VPC, and experienced the importance of teamwork and resilience while working under intense time pressure during a real-world hackathon.
